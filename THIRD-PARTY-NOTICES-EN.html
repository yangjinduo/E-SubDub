IV. Build Tools (Not Distributed)

The E-SubDub installer is compiled with Nuitka (AGPL-3.0, build-time only, not part of the distribution) and packaged with Inno Setup. According to the official Nuitka statement, programs built with Nuitka do not statically or dynamically link against Nuitka, and are therefore not required to open their source code.
